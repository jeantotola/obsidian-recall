## Tie-breaks

One question per pair of folders that could both take a page.

## New folders

- Prefer an existing folder or subfolder.
- New subfolder: only for a recognized subarea of its folder, such as a chapter in a textbook on the subject.
- New top-level folder: only when at least 3 pages that fit no folder are expected, and the subject is not a subarea of an existing folder.
- A new top-level folder gets its row in the table above in the same commit as its first page.
